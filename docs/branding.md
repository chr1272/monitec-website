Based on your logo design, here is a complete visual brand identity and design system for **Monitec**. 

This system bridges the two key concepts in your logo: **Telematics / Vehicles (the road)** and **Data Analysis (the bar charts & graph nodes)**.

---

Your logo establishes a high-trust, technical, and modern palette.

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | RGB | Visual Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

* **30% Structure:** `#071C33` & `#0F75BC` (Navbar, headers, major UI components)
* **10% Energy:** `#00C1D5` (Primary CTA buttons, interactive hover states, key data highlights)

# 2. Typography System

To mirror the clean, geometric structure of your logo, use open-source Google Fonts:

### **Primary Display / Headings: Montserrat**
* Matches the logo's geometric sans-serif weight and tracking.
* **H1 / H2:** Bold (`700`), Dark Navy (`#071C33`)
* **Taglines & Subheaders:** Medium (`500`) or Light (`300`), Uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`letter-spacing: 2px`).

### **Body & UI Components: Inter**
* Highly legible at small scales, ideal for dashboards, telemetry data tables, and web copy.
* **Body Text:** Regular (`400`), Slate (`#1A2332`), Line Height: `1.6`.
* **Buttons / Navigation:** SemiBold (`600`).

### **Data & Metrics (Telemetry Tables): JetBrains Mono**
* Monospaced font for vehicle speeds, timestamps, fuel levels, and GPS coordinates to align columns perfectly.

---

# 3. Form & Shape Scheme (Design Language)

* **Card Radius:** **`8px` to `12px`** — Softened geometric corners for dashboard cards and UI containers.
* **Buttons:** **`6px` radius or Pill-shaped (`24px`)**. Primary button: Solid `#00C1D5` with dark text `#071C33` for high visibility, or Deep Navy background with Cyan border/text.
* **Graph & Connected Node Pattern:** Use subtle thin cyan lines connecting small dots (`#00C1D5`) as dynamic background vector overlays for Hero sections and feature blocks.
* **The "Road Swoosh":** Use curved section dividers on the website (15°–20° subtle angular cuts or smooth swooshes) to evoke momentum and motion.

# 4. Business Card (*Visitenkarte*) Concept

**Dimensions:** Standard 85 × 54 mm (Horizontal)

### **Front Side (Dark & Premium)**
* **Background:** Deep Navy Blue (`#071C33`)
* **Logo:** Centered full logo with Bright Cyan domain suffix (`monitec.io`, `.at`, or `.fr` depending on regional branch).
* **Finish:** Soft-touch matte dark finish with Spot UV gloss on the road icon and logo text.

### **Back Side (Clean & Structured)**
* **Background:** Clean White (`#FFFFFF`) with a subtle Deep Navy bottom edge bar.
* **Layout:** Two-column grid.
  * **Left Column:** 
    * Name (**Montserrat Bold**, Navy `#071C33`)
    * Title / Position (**Inter Regular**, Medium Blue `#0F75BC`)
    * Small cyan dot (`#00C1D5`) accent separator.
  * **Right Column (Icons in Cyan):**
    * Phone, Email, Address, Web URL.
* **Visual Detail:** A faint watermark pattern of the line-graph nodes running diagonally across the top right corner.

# 5. Website UI/UX Direction

### **Header & Navigation**
* Clean white or semi-transparent dark navy navbar sticky on scroll.
* **Language/Region Switcher:** A dropdown allowing toggling between `.io` (Global/EN), `.at` (Austria/DE), and `.fr` (France/FR) which automatically updates the logo's domain suffix and translated tagline.

### **Hero Section (Above the Fold)**
* **Background:** Dark Navy (`#071C33`) with subtle animated graph nodes connecting in the background.
* **Headline:** *"Real-time Telemetry Data & Fleet Intelligence"*
* **CTA Buttons:** 
  * Primary: Bright Cyan (`#00C1D5`) — *"Request Demo"*
  * Secondary: Outline Blue — *"Explore Platform"*
* **Hero Visual:** An interactive 3D/Isometric UI mockup showing a live vehicle map alongside live metric charts (speed, fuel, telemetry logs).

### **Feature Grid (Telemetry Capabilities)**
* **Data Cards:** Light background (`#F4F7FA`) with 1px border (`#E2E8F0`).
* **Hover State:** Border turns Cyan (`#00C1D5`), card lifts slightly (`transform: translateY(-4px)`), subtle blue shadow.
* **Iconography:** Line-art icons using Cyan and Ocean Blue matching the logo's graph nodes.

# 6. Business & Legal Constraints

- **Type:** Diagnostic Telemetry & Decision-Support System (System zur Entscheidungsgrundlage / Outil d'aide à la décision).
- **Deliverable:** Automated PDF & Web Analysis Report (Vehicle counts, speed distribution, acoustic frequency spectrum, weather correlation).

- **Input:** 12V DC input.
- **Power Options:** Includes standard 230V-to-12V socket adapter. Compatible with 12V battery and solar setups.
- **Shipment:** Shipped directly to customer via carrier; self-installation & return box.

### Compliance & Privacy
- **Standards:** Full GDPR (EU) and CNIL (France) compliance.
- **Data Privacy:** 100% anonymized statistical data. No video/facial recognition, no license plate storage, no audio wiretapping.

### Target Markets & Language Mapping

- **Austria (`monitec.at`):** DE language, Austrian municipal context (Gemeinden, DSGVO).
